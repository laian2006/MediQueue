## Scope

Implement:

POST /api/v1/queue/tickets/{ticketId}/no-show

The endpoint accepts no request body or query parameters.

## Acceptance criteria

- Only authenticated STAFF accounts can mark a ticket absent.
- The ticket must be CALLED.
- The session must be OPEN or STOPPED.
- The timeout comes from NO_SHOW_TIMEOUT_SECONDS.
- Time is measured from the latest lastCalledAt.
- Requests before the deadline return 409 NO_SHOW_TOO_EARLY.
- Early responses include details.remainingSeconds, rounded up.
- Requests exactly at or after the deadline can succeed.
- Recall starts a new timeout window.
- Successful requests preserve ticket identity and lastCalledAt.
- The ticket becomes NO_SHOW and releases the counter.
- The NO_SHOW event and ticket update share one transaction.
- Session locking precedes ticket locking.
- Concurrent operations cannot create conflicting transitions.
- No background process automatically marks tickets absent.
